# Keywordia content sources

Prepared and source-checked: 2026-10-15. Expanded and source-checked: 2026-10-16 (items 11 to 20 and number items 03 and 04 in every topic), then expanded again the same day (items 21 to 50 and number items 05 and 06 in every topic).

The bank holds 300 multiple-choice questions and 36 "closest guess" number questions across six topics: fifty multiple-choice and six number questions per topic. The questions are written for a general marketing audience: small business owners, freelancers and agency people who work with Google, search and clients. Each question has four distinct options, one correct answer, a short explanation and a public source link.

The six topic ids are `seo` (Google Search), `ads` (Google Ads), `content` (Content & YouTube), `local` (Google Maps & Reviews), `ai` (AI Tools) and `clients` (Clients & Agency).

In conquest, every third round is a closest guess question while unused ones remain in the selected topics; after that the slots fall back to multiple choice. Blitz uses multiple choice only. Items never repeat within a match.

## Source rules

- Every item is backed by a public primary source that was opened during writing: official Google, Google Ads, Business Profile, Maps and YouTube help pages, Google Search Central, Google's and YouTube's own about and blog pages, Anthropic's official Claude pages (AI Tools topic only), or the public AgriciDaniel and AI-Marketing-Hub GitHub repositories.
- Every evidence quote added on 2026-10-16 was matched word for word against a download of its source page made that day. For items 21 to 50 and number items 05 and 06, the 63 quotes from Search Central, YouTube, Claude and GitHub pages were rechecked live after writing. Google's help center (support.google.com) started refusing automated requests partway through, so the other 129 quotes were matched against the copies of those help pages saved earlier that day, not a second live download.
- No blogs, news sites, Wikipedia or vendor pages are cited.
- No private community material, member content, prompt catalogs, client data or credentials were used.
- Questions and explanations are original wording. The quotes below are short excerpts kept only as review evidence.
- Number questions use values stated directly in the source (a launch year or a documented limit). They are not calculations.
